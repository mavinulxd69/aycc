What Stays the Same

Open field, no brackets

Every school, college, and university student in Bangladesh competes on the same board — no age or gender divisions.

Free pre-registration

Pre-registration stays free, with a nominal fee for late entries so the tournament remains accessible to every student.

Merit-only judging

Only the moves on the board decide who rises — the same qualifier-to-top-32 format that ran at AYCT 26.

A new city

AYCT moves venues each year so more players across Bangladesh get a seat at the table. Host city for 2027: to be announced.
